# Contributing to Nhat Nguyet

Thank you for helping. These notes apply to every repository that has no `CONTRIBUTING.md` of its own; read that file first when it exists.

## Ways to help

- **Report a wrong result.** Give the input, what you got, what you expected, and your source: a book with chapter and page, or astronomical data with a link. A result that disagrees with one school but follows another is usually a school option, not a bug.
- **Suggest a feature.** Describe the problem you want solved before the solution.
- **Improve a translation.** We publish in English, Chinese and Vietnamese; fixes from native speakers are welcome.
- **Send a pull request.** For anything larger than a typo, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.

## Pull requests

- One topic per pull request, linked to its issue ("Fixes #123").
- Run the repository's tests and paste the pass counts.
- Keep public text plain: short sentences, no hype words. Brand name: "Nhat Nguyet" in English and Chinese, "Nhật Nguyệt" in Vietnamese.
- Issues and pull requests may be written in English, Vietnamese or Chinese.

## Conduct

Everyone taking part follows our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Questions

Use the Discussions tab of the repository, or email lienhe@nhatnguyet.org.
